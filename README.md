# About the project

This hospital reception management system is designed for small to medium sized healthcare facilities to organize daily operations. It acts as a digital front desk to streamline patient management and reduces reliance on manual record keeping, allowing staff to quickly access to essential patient information during busy clinical hours. The interface is clean, user-friendly, and supports smoother workflows for administrative teams.

## Overview

AVTech Hospital Reception System gives hospital staff a central place to:
- log in securely to the system
- view the main dashboard with patient and claim summaries
- search for existing patients
- register new patient records
- manage patient admissions
- schedule appointments
- store medical reports
- record insurance or claim details

The overall goal is to make hospital administration smoother, faster, and more accurate.

## Features

### 1. Login Page
The login page is the entry point of the hospital system. It gives staff a simple and professional login interface before accessing the dashboard. This helps protect patient information and keeps the hospital system secure and organized.

### 2. Homepage
The homepage acts as the main control panel for administrators, and it provides a quick overview of important hospital data with:
- total registered patients
- admitted patients
- appointments
- insurance claims

A patient search feature is also included for faster patients record retrieval by ID or name.

### 3. Patient Registration
This feature helps to add new patients into the system, with clean and organized patient database for future visit and hospital procedures. Each record stores personal details such as name, age, date of birth, gender, height, weight, address, postal code, phone number, and email. 

### 4. Patient Admission
This feature helps to manage hospital admissions for smooth patient intake and keep room assignments organized. Staff can select a patient, choose the admission date, assign a doctor, decide the admission type, and allocate a room based on availability. 

### 5. Appointment Scheduling
The scheduling system plans appointments for patients in a weekly calendar view. With a useful time-slot layout, patient visits are arranged efficiently. This helps reduce confusion, avoids scheduling overlaps, and keeps the clinic workflow well managed.

### 6. Billing and Insurance Claims
The billing section is designed to record insurance claims, where staff can add claim data such as policy number, patient details, contact information, admission dates, physician name, hospital name, and the claim amount.

### 7. Medical Report Management
This feature lets staff create and track patient medical records. It stores patient information such as blood type, weight, height, assessments, illnesses, treatments, prescriptions and physician details. This is important for maintaining accurate medical histories and supporting treatment decisions.
